1. Overview & Commitment

Raju's Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the KhataFlow mobile application (package: com.khataflow.ledger). We are firmly committed to safeguarding the privacy and financial confidential records of every merchant, business owner, customer, and supplier using our platform.

KhataFlow is built upon an offline-first, zero-tracking architecture. Your business transactions, ledger accounts, cashbook registers, and invoices belong solely to you and are stored locally on your device by default.

2. Information We Process & Purpose

KhataFlow processes only the minimum data required to deliver core bookkeeping, inventory management, and POS billing services:

A. Ledger, Transactions & Business Records

  • Customer & Supplier Accounts: Names, contact numbers, and optional billing addresses entered by you.
  • Financial Transactions: Credit (You Gave) and Debit (You Got) amounts, dates, notes, and payment methods (Cash, Online, UPI).
  • Inventory & Items: Stock names, unit types, purchase/sale rates, GST tax slabs, and quantities.
  • Daily Cashbook: Daily cash-in and cash-out entries for physical cash drawer reconciliation.
  • Invoices & Receipts: Business header details (Store Name, GSTIN, Phone, Address) displayed on printed or exported PDF/Excel bills.
🔒 Private Local Storage:

All ledger records and transactions are saved locally on your device in secure, sandboxed application storage. We do not sell or monetize your financial information.

B. Android Device Permissions (Optional & User-Initiated)

KhataFlow requests explicit permissions strictly when you choose to use specific app features:

  • Contacts (Optional): Allows you to pick a customer or supplier directly from your phone address book when creating a ledger entry, preventing manual typing mistakes. Your contact book is never uploaded or transmitted to any server.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby Devices (Optional): Required solely to discover and connect with 58mm or 80mm wireless Bluetooth thermal printers for generating physical POS tax receipts.
  • Storage & Files (Optional): Used only when exporting balance sheets (PDF / Excel), saving invoice receipts to device storage, or attaching payment proof photos.
  • Phone Call Intent (Optional): Allows one-tap calling to customers or suppliers directly via your default phone dialer. KhataFlow does not record calls or access call history.

C. Automated Payment Reminders

When you tap Remind to send a payment reminder via WhatsApp or SMS, KhataFlow triggers your device's native WhatsApp or SMS application with pre-drafted reminder details and dynamic UPI payment links. KhataFlow never reads your private chats or SMS messages.

3. What We Never Do (Privacy Guarantee)

  • We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • We NEVER collect continuous real-time GPS location tracking.
  • We NEVER inspect bank credentials, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords.
  • We NEVER place invasive third-party ad tracking SDKs inside your business books.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

KhataFlow interfaces with certified, industry-standard infrastructure providers to ensure high stability:

  • Google Play Services: Core application delivery, security verification, and automatic updates. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase / Cloud Sync (If Enabled): Optional encrypted cloud backup, authentication, and crash telemetry. (Firebase Privacy Policy)

All transmission of data between the app and external services is protected using TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols.

5. Data Security

Your data security is paramount. Local databases are protected within the sandboxed application container provided by the Android OS, preventing unauthorized access by other installed applications.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You retain full rights to your data at all times:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can wipe all local records immediately at any time by navigating to Settings > Apps > KhataFlow > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Cloud Account Deletion: If you registered an optional cloud backup account and wish to permanently erase your records, backups, and profile from cloud servers, send an email to rajvadaviya1@gmail.com with the subject line "KhataFlow Data Deletion Request". Your account and all stored records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

KhataFlow is designed as a business accounting and invoicing utility for merchants and commercial users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The current version will always remain accessible on this publicly available page with an updated "Effective Date".

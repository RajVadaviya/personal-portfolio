## 2. Information We Handle and Why

KhataFlow processes only the essential information necessary to perform bookkeeping, billing, inventory tracking, and payment reminders:

### A. Business & Customer Ledger Data
* **Party Profiles:** Names, phone numbers, and optional billing addresses of your customers and suppliers.
* **Transaction Records:** Credit (You Gave / दिया) and Debit (You Got / लिया) amounts, transaction dates, descriptions, payment modes (Cash, Online, UPI, Bank), and optional receipt attachments.
* **Product & Inventory Details:** Item names, purchase prices, sales prices, GST tax rates, unit measurements, and stock quantities.
* **Daily Cashbook (गल्ला):** Daily cash-in, cash-out, and daily drawer reconciliation tallies.
* **Invoices & Receipts:** Itemized billing invoices, invoice serial numbers, and business header details (Business Name, Address, GSTIN, Phone).

> **Storage Model:** All ledger entries, products, and transaction records are stored **locally on your device in secure sandboxed application storage**. They are never sold or shared with any advertising brokers.

### B. Device Permissions (Optional & Feature-Specific)
KhataFlow requests explicit permissions only when you interact with specific features:
* **Contacts (`READ_CONTACTS` - Optional):** Used solely to allow you to conveniently pick an existing customer or supplier name and phone number from your contact list when creating a ledger account, avoiding manual typing errors. Your contact list is never uploaded, scanned, or transmitted to any external server.
* **Bluetooth / Nearby Devices (`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` - Optional):** Required exclusively to discover and connect to external 58mm or 80mm Bluetooth thermal POS receipt printers.
* **Storage / Photos / Files (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` - Optional):** Required only when you choose to export audit reports (PDF / Excel balance sheets), save invoice receipts to your device, or attach bill photos.
* **Phone Call (`CALL_PHONE` - Optional):** Used only when you tap the "Call" button on a customer profile to launch your phone's default dialer. KhataFlow does not record calls or access call logs.

### C. Automated Payment Reminders (WhatsApp & SMS)
When sending automated payment reminders or dynamic UPI payment links to customers, KhataFlow utilizes the standard Android Intent system to open your installed WhatsApp or default SMS app with pre-filled reminder text. **KhataFlow does not read your private chat messages or SMS inbox.**

### D. Technical & Diagnostic Information
To ensure app performance, security, and stability:
* Standard crash reports, device model, operating system version, and general non-identifying diagnostic data may be collected via Google Play Services for debugging and quality assurance.
